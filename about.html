What is FitUp?

FitUp is a free, personal fitness tracking web application that helps you follow structured workout programs, log exercises, track nutrition, and monitor your progress over time.

Features

  • 48-Week Training Program: Structured progressive overload workout plan with detailed exercise instructions and video demos.
  • Workout Logging: Track sets, reps, weights, and tempo for every exercise.
  • AI Nutrition Analysis: Snap a photo of your meal and get instant nutritional breakdown powered by Google Gemini AI.
  • Cloud Sync: Sign in with Google to backup and sync your data across devices via Google Drive.
  • Multi-Language Support: Available in English, Hebrew, and Arabic.
  • Offline Support: Works as a Progressive Web App (PWA) — use it even without internet.

How We Use Google Services

FitUp uses Google Sign-In to authenticate users and Google Drive to store a backup of your fitness data as a JSON file. We only access files created by the app. Your data remains private and is never shared with third parties.
