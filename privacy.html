Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

FitUp is a personal fitness tracking application. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

  • Fitness Data: Workout logs, exercise sets, reps, weights, and meal/nutrition notes you enter into the app.
  • Google Account Info: When you sign in with Google, we access your name, email, and profile picture solely for authentication and display purposes.
  • Google Drive: We store your fitness data as a JSON file in your Google Drive for cloud backup and cross-device sync.

How We Use Your Data

  • To display and track your workouts and nutrition.
  • To sync your data across devices via Google Drive.
  • To provide AI-powered nutritional analysis (via Google Gemini API) when you choose to use it.

Data Storage

All fitness data is stored locally on your device using IndexedDB. If you enable cloud sync, a copy is stored in your personal Google Drive account. We do not store your data on any external server.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Identity Services: For authentication.
  • Google Drive API: For cloud backup/sync.
  • Google Gemini API: For optional AI-powered meal analysis (only when you provide an API key).

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal data to any third parties. Your data stays on your device and in your personal Google Drive.

Your Rights

  • You can delete all your data at any time from the app settings.
  • You can revoke Google access at Google Account Permissions.
  • You can export your data from the app at any time.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact us at aboglion@gmail.com.
