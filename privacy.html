FitUp - Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

Overview

FitUp ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our personal fitness tracking web application collects, uses, and safeguards your data when you use FitUp.

Data We Collect & How We Use It

  • Fitness & Workout Data: Sets, reps, weights, exercise logs, and nutritional notes entered directly into FitUp.
  • Google Account Profile: Name, email address, and profile photo when signing in with Google OAuth for device authentication.
  • Google Drive Storage: FitUp creates and reads a single fitup-data.json backup file in your personal Google Drive account to sync your fitness logs across your devices.
  • Google Fit Data: Read-only access to daily step counts, expended activity calories, and heart rate data from Google Fit to calculate and display your personal daily energy balance.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

FitUp's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs (Google Drive API and Google Fit API) to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data Storage & Security

All fitness data is saved locally on your device via browser IndexedDB. If you connect Google Sign-In, backup copies are synced directly to your personal Google Drive account. We do not store or transmit your fitness data to any external third-party server.

Data Sharing & Third Parties

FitUp does not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information or health metrics with third parties or advertising networks.

Your Data Rights

  • You can delete all stored data from the app settings at any time.
  • You can revoke FitUp's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Permissions.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at aboglion@gmail.com.
